Product Description

The CubeMars AK70-10 KV100 robotic actuator integrates a high-performance brushless DC motor, 10:1 planetary gearbox, driver board, and encoder into a compact, easy-to-install unit. Featuring a 10:1 gear reduction, it delivers increased torque output while maintaining fast response times. The module supports multi-loop control (position, speed, torque, and MIT hybrid mode), includes plug-and-play connectors and auto PID parameter identification, and features built-in protection against overcurrent, overvoltage/undervoltage, and overheating for stable operation in demanding environments.
